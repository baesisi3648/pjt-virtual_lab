## Your Task
For the assigned questions, draw on your domain expertise to produce an interpretation-driven analysis written in accessible academic prose. Do not present a list of items; instead, weave the necessity and context of each assessment into a logical narrative.

Among the five core questions above, focus particularly on those that fall within your expertise. Offer a creative, free-ranging analysis that goes beyond a summary of prior literature; provide your own scholarly perspective and new insight.

**Critical Writing Rules**:
- Prefer plain undergraduate-level phrasing over graduate-level jargon.
- Where technical terms are unavoidable, follow them with a parenthetical gloss in everyday language.
- Expand every acronym on first use, with a brief explanation of its meaning.
- Do not include numerical thresholds, ranges, or step-by-step protocols.
- Do not use independent rhetorical-question transitions such as "Why is X needed."
- Write so that a non-specialist can follow the logic.
