[Research Agenda — Five Core Questions for an NGT Food Safety Assessment Framework]

This study aims to derive a standardised safety assessment framework for foods produced by New Genomic Techniques (NGT) through systematic, multidisciplinary deliberation. Specialist agents address the following five core questions.

## Background
The rapid emergence of New Genomic Techniques (NGT) — including CRISPR–Cas, base editors, prime editors, and oligonucleotide-directed mutagenesis — requires a systematic re-examination of whether existing risk-assessment frameworks for genetically modified organisms (GMOs) remain adequate. The objective is a science-based, proportionate, and category-level assessment scaffold.

## Five Core Questions

### Q1. Risk Identification
For foods and feeds derived from NGT (SDN-1, SDN-2, SDN-3, ODM), what newly identifiable potential hazards or risks emerge relative to established genetic engineering techniques (EGT) and conventional breeding?

### Q2. Comparative Risk Analysis
How do process-associated risks (off-target events, structural rearrangements, residual process elements) and outcome-associated risks (allergenicity, toxicity, nutritional alteration) compare systematically across NGT, EGT, and conventional breeding? What are the points of convergence and divergence?

### Q3. Applicability of Existing Guidelines
Are the six pillars of existing GMO risk-assessment guidance (recipient information, molecular characterisation, comparative analysis, toxicology, allergenicity, nutrition) — as embodied in Codex GL44/GL45 and EFSA opinions — directly applicable to NGT foods, partially applicable, or in need of new components?

### Q4. Direction of Guideline Updates
For areas where existing guidance fails to reflect NGT-specific characteristics, what concrete supplementation, revision, or addition is warranted? Can a one-to-one mapping be drawn between identified hazards/methodological gaps and corresponding solutions anchored in existing international vocabularies?

### Q5. Integrated Decision Framework
Synthesising the above, how should a stepwise, proportionate decision workflow — including category-specific decision trees for SDN-1, SDN-2, SDN-3, and ODM — be constructed?

## Mapping of Questions to Report Sections
- Q1, Q2 → Part 1: Identification of new potential hazards and risks
- Q3 → Part 2: Applicability and sufficiency of existing guidance
- Q4 → Part 3: Items requiring update, revision, or supplementation
